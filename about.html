How NIMS Tatkal Seva App Works

A simple 6-step workflow designed to deliver emergency and routine healthcare without waiting lines.

1

1. Download the App

Download the NIMS Tatkal Seva app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

2

2. Register / Login

Create your account using your mobile number and complete the basic details.

3

3. Choose a Service

Select the service you need — ICU Bed, OPD, Appointment, Ambulance, Health Checkup, Home Sample Collection, or Health Package.

4

4. Enter Required Details

Provide the required patient and booking information.

5

5. Confirm Your Request

Review the details and submit your booking/request.

6

6. Get Updates

Receive booking confirmations and important updates through the app.
