2. Scope of Services

NIMS Tatkal Seva connects patients and their caregivers with hospital healthcare infrastructure, facilitating:

  • ICU Bed Booking Requests: Digital checking and request submission for critical care and Intensive Care Unit (ICU) beds.
  • OPD Consultations & Online Appointments: Scheduling in-person and digital consultations with certified doctors and medical specialists.
  • Ambulance Transportation: Requesting emergency and patient transfer ambulances equipped with paramedic and life-support staff.
  • Health Checkups & Preventive Packages: Exploration and booking of comprehensive wellness packages.
  • Home Sample Collection: Booking certified phlebotomists for diagnostic and laboratory sample collection from residential addresses.
  • Health Packages: Curated diagnostic, surgical, and therapeutic healthcare packages.

3. Bed Availability & ICU Booking Protocol

Submitting an ICU Bed Booking request through the app does not guarantee instantaneous bed allotment until verified and confirmed by NIMS Hospital's Clinical Triage and Bed Management Department.

Critical care bed allotment is subject to patient medical acuity, doctor assessment, availability of specialized equipment (e.g., mechanical ventilators, ECMO, dialysis support), and verification of patient records upon arrival.

4. User Registration and Account Security

To access booking services, users must register using a valid mobile number and provide accurate information including patient full name, age, gender, contact number, and medical condition details.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of any one-time passwords (OTP) or authentication credentials sent to your device. Any activity originating from your verified account will be deemed authorized by you.

5. Ambulance Dispatch and Transportation

Ambulance requests dispatched via NIMS Tatkal Seva are operated under hospital emergency management protocols:

  • Accurate location coordinates and landmark details must be provided by the caller.
  • Estimated Arrival Times (ETA) are subject to traffic conditions, weather, and distance from NIMS Hospital or dispatch hubs.
  • NIMS Hospital's emergency team reserves the right to prioritize cases according to medical urgency (triaging).

6. Appointments, OPD Consultations & Cancellations

Doctor availability is subject to emergency surgeries and hospital rounds. In the event of emergency unavailability of a scheduled doctor, NIMS Hospital reserves the right to reschedule the appointment or offer consultation with an equivalent specialist.

Cancellations or rescheduling of appointments must be made at least two (2) hours prior to the scheduled slot via the app or hospital helpline.

7. Home Diagnostic Sample Collection

Home sample collections are conducted by trained phlebotomists adhering to sterile laboratory standards. Patients are responsible for observing necessary preparation instructions (e.g., fasting, medication restrictions) communicated prior to sample collection. Diagnostic test reports are generated under standard laboratory turnaround times.
