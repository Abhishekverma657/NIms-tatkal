PATIENT DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Commitment to Patient Privacy

NIMS Tatkal Seva, operating as an official digital healthcare platform and unit of NIMS Hospital, Jaipur ("NIMS Hospital", "we", "us", or "our"), is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our patients and app users.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal and medical information is collected, processed, stored, and protected when you use our mobile application and related web services (nimshospitals.net).

MEDICAL CONFIDENTIALITY PROMISE: We treat all health-related and medical consultation data with the strictest standards of clinical confidentiality. Your medical records are never sold, rented, or distributed to commercial advertisers.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide rapid healthcare bookings and emergency assistance, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identification Details: Patient full name, age, gender, primary mobile number, email address, and home or pickup address.
  • Healthcare & Booking Data: Requested medical service (ICU bed booking, doctor OPD specialty, ambulance pickup, preventive checkup, or home diagnostic sample collection), relevant symptoms, and patient health notes submitted during booking.
  • Location Information: Real-time GPS location data when you request ambulance pickup or home phlebotomy visits, ensuring accurate routing and minimal response delays.
  • Technical & Device Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and app performance logs to diagnose technical errors.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for clinical and operational healthcare functions:

  • To schedule, confirm, and manage ICU bed requests, doctor appointments, and OPD visits.
  • To dispatch ambulance emergency teams to your exact coordinates with paramedic teams.
  • To coordinate phlebotomist home visits for diagnostic blood, urine, or pathology collections.
  • To deliver diagnostic laboratory reports and booking confirmation SMS or app notifications.
  • To comply with statutory healthcare guidelines, medical recordkeeping protocols, and government health mandates.

4. Use of Device Location Services

The NIMS Tatkal Seva app requests access to your device location services solely when necessary for:

  • Accurate ambulance emergency routing to the patient's current physical location.
  • Enabling home sample collection technicians to locate residential pickup addresses without delay.

You may control or disable location permissions in your mobile device settings, though disabling location may necessitate manual address typing during emergency ambulance calls.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We only share your information with parties directly involved in your care:

  • Hospital Medical Staff: Treating doctors, clinical triage nurses, ICU coordinators, and hospital administrative staff at NIMS Hospital, Jaipur.
  • Emergency Personnel: Paramedics, ambulance drivers, and emergency response dispatchers.
  • Diagnostic Laboratory Team: NIMS Hospital accredited lab technicians and certified phlebotomists.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly mandated by applicable Indian laws, judicial court orders, or statutory healthcare reporting regulations.

6. Data Security and Encryption

We maintain stringent technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal and medical information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss:

  • End-to-end SSL/TLS cryptographic encryption for all network transmissions.
  • Secure cloud and server infrastructure with restricted role-based administrative access.
  • Periodic vulnerability assessments, firewalls, and data access audits.

7. Retention of Health Records

Personal and health information is retained in accordance with the Indian Medical Council (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations and applicable digital healthcare retention guidelines to ensure continuous clinical continuity for future treatments.

8. Your Rights as a Patient

You have the right to review, update, or request corrections to your personal information recorded in your account. You may also request deletion of non-clinical account credentials by submitting a verified request to our administrative desk, subject to statutory healthcare record preservation laws.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in technology, service features, or applicable legal standards. Any revisions will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
